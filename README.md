## 💧 Website Section: "Every Drop Counts"## 1. Landing Page Overview
Empower your home, track your usage, and build a sustainable community. This interactive platform helps individuals and residential societies measure, understand, and reduce their daily water footprints through data-driven tracking and real-time visualization.
------------------------------
## 2. Personal Water Footprint Calculator (Per Person)
This interactive module calculates estimated daily consumption based on individual habits.
## User Inputs:

* Shower Time: Slider (minutes per day)
* Flush Type: Choice (Standard 3-gallon vs. Dual-flush eco-friendly)
* Dishwashing: Choice (Hand washing with running tap vs. Efficient dishwasher)
* Laundry Loads: Number (per week)

## Live Feedback Metrics:

* Your Daily Total: [X] Gallons / Liters per day.
* The Benchmark: A direct comparison against the national average (e.g., 80–100 gallons per person per day).
* Eco-Status Badge: Dynamic rating from 🔴 High Consumer to 🟢 Water Saver.

------------------------------
## 3. Society & Community Analytics Dashboard
This section aggregates data to give residential societies, apartment complexes, or neighborhoods a macro-view of their collective consumption.
## Suggested Graphs & Visualizations:

| Graph Type | Data Displayed | Core Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Stacked Bar Chart | Monthly consumption broken down by building, block, or wing. | Identifies which sectors or buildings are consuming the most water to target awareness campaigns. |
| Line Graph | Daily society water usage plotted against the municipal target line. | Tracks progress over time and monitors whether conservation goals are being met. |
| Pie Chart | Total society water breakdown by use-case (e.g., Domestic, Landscaping, Swimming Pool, Car Washing). | Reveals hidden leaks or heavy operational waste areas (e.g., over-watering lawns). |

## Public Leaderboard Widget:

* "Top Conserving Wings" – A friendly competition tracker showing which apartment block reduced their consumption the most compared to the previous month.

------------------------------
## 4. Community Action & Impact Section

* Smart Alerts: Instant notifications if the society graph detects a sudden spike in baseline usage, signaling a potential main-line leak.
* Resource Library: Downloadable guidelines for rainwater harvesting setups, greywater recycling, and local plumber contacts specializing in low-flow aerator installations.

mart Water Conservation Website

This repository contains a simple static website for a smart water conservation project.

## Features
- Responsive landing page
- Water conservation messaging and benefits
- Savings calculator powered by JavaScript
- Modern green-themed design

## Run locally
Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with a local web server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

Then open `http://localhost:8000`.

## Files
- `index.html` — page structure
- `styles.css` — styling and layout
- `script.js` — savings calculator logic
